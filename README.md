## Repository Structure

### `mr_analyses/`

Mendelian randomization analyses examining the relationship between maternal pre-pregnancy BMI and offspring metabolic outcomes.

### `prs_construct/`

Scripts for constructing maternal and child BMI polygenic risk scores (PRS).

### `phasing/`

Scripts related to genetic phasing and preparation of genotype data for downstream analyses.

### `longitudinal_analyses/`

Analyses examining offspring outcomes across longitudinal measurements (LMM and GEE models).

### `sensitivity_analyses/`

Additional analyses used to assess the sensitivity of the main findings.
